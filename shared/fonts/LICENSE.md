| Family | Source | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Press Start 2P | Google Fonts (CodeMan38) | `press-start-2p-*.woff2` |
| Orbitron | Google Fonts (Matt McInerney) | `orbitron-latin.woff2` |
| DotGothic16 | Google Fonts (Fontworks Inc.) | `dotgothic16-*.woff2` |
| M PLUS Rounded 1c | Google Fonts (Coji Morishita) | `mplus-rounded-1c-*.woff2` |
| Patrick Hand | Google Fonts (Patrick Wagesreiter) | `patrick-hand-*.woff2` |

Only the `latin` and `latin-ext` subsets are shipped. They are served from
this origin instead of `fonts.gstatic.com` so that no visitor IP address is
transmitted to Google without consent (GDPR).
